About BRIDGE

A directory designed to be honest.

BRIDGE is a lightweight research interface for cross-border B2B discovery. It does not claim to represent the companies listed, certify them, or broker an introduction.

Use the public information here to form a shortlist, then verify ownership, licensing, product fit, trade terms, and contacts with the organization directly.

01

Source over spectacle

Company names and descriptions are paired with an official website instead of unsupported “network” numbers.

02

No implied endorsement

A listing means only that the organization has a public web presence relevant to the category shown.

03

Privacy by default

No private email addresses, personal phone numbers, fabricated representatives, or fake messaging features are included.
